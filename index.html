An 8-bit jungle game for kids — jump, collect, count!

MINX'S BANANA JUMP

Help Minx the monkey collect bananas!
Minx runs forward all by himself — you just press SPACE (or tap) to jump.
Level 1 is just jumping. Level 2 adds swinging vines.
Every 3 bananas = 1 extra life. Count along: 1, 2, 3!

Minx runs forward on his own  •  The one key you need — Jump / let go of a vine: SPACE (or click / tap)  •  Grab a vine: jump toward it  •  Restart level: R
