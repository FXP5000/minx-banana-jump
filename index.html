An 8-bit jungle game for kids — jump, collect, count!

MINX'S BANANA JUMP

Help Minx the monkey collect bananas!
Level 1 is just jumping. Level 2 adds swinging vines.
Every 3 bananas = 1 extra life. Count along: 1, 2, 3!

Move: ◀ ▶ or A D  •  Jump / let go of a vine: SPACE  •  Grab a vine: walk into it  •  Restart level: R
