MINX'S BANANA JUMP

Help Minx the monkey collect bananas!
You control Minx: move him with ◀ ▶ (or A / D) and jump with SPACE.
The screen follows him forwards — but it never scrolls back, so there's no going back to the start!
Level 1 is just jumping. Level 2 adds swinging vines. Level 3 is the Tunnel Tree.
Every 3 bananas = 1 extra life. Count along: 1, 2, 3!
Beat all three levels to unlock the Endless Level — how high can you score?

MINX'S MAIN MENU

Pick your monkey and a hat! Everything is in rows — scroll the page to see them all.
Collect bananas in the game to fill your Banana Bank.
The cooler it looks, the more bananas it costs — the Rainbow monkey is 1000!
And check back every day for the Skin of the Day.

HATS & ACCESSORIES

Move: ◀ ▶ (or A / D)  •  Jump / let go of a vine: SPACE (or click / tap)  •  Grab a vine: jump toward it  •  Restart level: R
